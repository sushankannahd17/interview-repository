## Part 2: Quick Start Instructions (Using Gamma App — 2 Minutes)

1. Go to **[gamma.app](https://gamma.app)** and sign in with Google (Free).
2. Click **"Create new with AI"** $\rightarrow$ select **"Text to Deck"** or **"Paste in text"**.
3. Select **Presentation** format (10 or 12 cards/slides).
4. **Copy the Master Prompt below** (from Part 3) and paste it into the prompt box.
5. Choose a theme:
   - Recommended themes: **"Midnight"**, **"Charcoal"**, **"Obsidian"**, or **"Electric"** (dark SaaS themes that match `uxpilot.ai` aesthetics).
6. Click **Generate**!
7. Once generated, click the **Share / Export** button at top-right $\rightarrow$ **"Export to PowerPoint (.pptx)"** or **"Export to PDF"**.

```text
Create a modern, high-impact 11-slide pitch presentation for a software engineering hackathon project named "InterviewRepo". 
Theme & Tone: Professional, high-tech, modern SaaS, enterprise software engineering (Deep indigo, slate, dark glassmorphism aesthetic).
Use clear headings, structured bullet points, key metric callouts, and clean layout recommendations for each slide.

### Slide 2: The Campus Placement Crisis (Problem Statement)
- Header: The Problem: Placement Preparation is Broken

  4. Ad-Hoc Mentorship: Placement cells rely on manual spreadsheets with no structured way to pair candidates with domain-specific mentors.
```

## Part 4: Presenter Tips for Slide Delivery

1. **Keep Slides Visual**: Do not read from the slides word-for-word. Let the slide display the architecture diagram or key numbers while you narrate the user story.
2. **Emphasize the Architecture**: Judges love seeing **Java 21, Spring Boot, Supabase, and PostgreSQL**. Spend at least 60 seconds on Slide 9 (System Architecture) and Slide 10 (Engineering Challenges).
3. **Transition Directly into the Live Demo**: At Slide 11, invite the judges to watch the live application running on `http://localhost:5173`.
